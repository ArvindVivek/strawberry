# Strawberry Support

Need help? Reach us through the [Kitchen Labs contact page](https://kitchenlabs-one.vercel.app/contact).
We usually reply within two days.

## Common questions

**What is Strawberry?**
A support inbox for a made-up airline, Larkspur Air. Each customer email gets a **triage**: a
topic (baggage, refund, delay and so on), how urgent it is, the airline policy rules that apply,
quoted in full, and a drafted reply you can edit and send.

**Is Larkspur Air real? Are these real customers?**
No. The airline, its policy, the customers and their bookings are all invented, so you can try
the app without anyone's real data.

**How do I try the AI?**
Tap **New ticket**, write a customer email (or tap **Fill in an example**), add it to the inbox,
then tap **Triage with AI**. The sample tickets already have their triage written, so they don't
use AI at all.

**It says "AI features are paused right now."**
The AI service is temporarily unavailable on our side. Strawberry still drafts the triage with its
built-in rules, without AI, so you can keep working; try AI again later.

**It says "That's a lot of tries in a row."**
Each visitor can run 5 AI triages a minute and 20 a day. Wait a moment, or use **Draft without
AI**, which has no limit.

**Does "Send reply" email anyone?**
No. It marks the reply as sent in your browser. Nothing leaves the page.

**Where are my tickets saved? How do I clear them?**
In your browser only. **Reset the sample inbox** (at the bottom of the list) removes the tickets
you wrote and restores the samples. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

**Can I read the whole policy?**
Yes: **Policy** in the top bar opens it. Every rule number in a triage links to its place there.
